Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between international and Korean age?

International age adds one year only after each birthday. Korean age starts at 1 at birth and increases by one on every January 1.

Did Korea officially switch to international age in 2023?

Yes. Since June 28, 2023, international age is the official legal and administrative standard in Korea. Korean age is still used in everyday conversation.

How is age calculated for people born on Feb 29 (leap day)?

In non-leap years, either Feb 28 or Mar 1 is recognized as the birthday. Legally, Feb 28 is typically used.
