Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert pyeong to square meters?

1 pyeong = 3.30579 m². A 33-pyeong apartment is about 109 m².

How many pyeong is 1 acre?

1 acre ≈ 1,224 pyeong (≈ 4,047 m²). Commonly used in US land transactions.

How do I convert square feet to square meters?

1 sq ft (ft²) ≈ 0.0929 m². 100 ft² ≈ 9.3 m².
