Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if the total doesn't divide evenly?

Any leftover cents (or won) from dividing the total by the number of people are automatically assigned one at a time to the first people in the list, so the total always adds up exactly.

How is the settle-up list calculated?

People who are owed money and people who owe money are matched largest-to-largest to reduce the number of transfers needed.

Is my data saved anywhere?

No. All calculations happen locally in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
