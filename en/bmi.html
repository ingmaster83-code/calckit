💡 BMI Classification (Korean Obesity Society)

  • Under 18.5: Underweight
  • 18.5 – 22.9: Normal
  • 23.0 – 24.9: Overweight
  • 25.0 – 29.9: Obese
  • 30.0 and above: Severely Obese

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the normal BMI range?

By WHO standards, 18.5–24.9 is normal; 25+ is overweight; 30+ is obese. In Korea, 23+ is sometimes classified as overweight.

Does a high BMI always mean I am obese?

Not necessarily. BMI cannot distinguish fat from muscle. Athletes with high muscle mass can have a high BMI. Use it as a general reference only.

How is ideal weight calculated?

Ideal weight (kg) = height (m)² × 22. For example, for 170 cm: 1.7² × 22 ≈ 63.6 kg.
