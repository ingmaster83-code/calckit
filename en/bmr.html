💡 BMR Notes

  • BMR: Minimum calories needed to sustain life at complete rest
  • TDEE: BMR × activity factor — total calories burned per day
  • Eating 500 kcal less per day results in ~0.5 kg weight loss per week
  • Uses Mifflin-St Jeor formula (widely considered the most accurate)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is basal metabolic rate (BMR)?

The minimum calories your body burns at complete rest to sustain life — used for breathing, maintaining body temperature, and organ function.

What is the BMR formula?

The Mifflin-St Jeor formula is the most accurate. Male: 10×weight(kg)+6.25×height(cm)−5×age+5; Female: 10×weight+6.25×height−5×age−161.

Will I lose weight if I eat less than my BMR?

You may lose weight short-term, but long-term it causes muscle loss and metabolic slowdown. Consult a professional before setting your calorie target.
