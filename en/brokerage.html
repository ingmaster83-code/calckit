Frequently Asked Questions

What are the maximum brokerage fee rates?

Revised from October 2021: purchase transactions have max rates of 0.4–0.7% depending on amount; jeonse/monthly rent have max rates of 0.3–0.6%.

Can the brokerage fee be negotiated?

Yes. You can negotiate below the maximum rate with the agent. Charging above the cap is illegal.

Are brokerage fees different for purchase vs. jeonse?

Yes. Purchase transactions have higher maximum rates than jeonse, and rates vary by transaction amount tier.
