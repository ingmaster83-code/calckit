💡 Tips

  • Average Korean domestic car depreciates ~15%/year. Expect an immediate 10–15% drop the moment you drive off the lot.
  • Actual depreciation varies widely by brand, model, color, and options. This is a general estimate.
  • Mileage and accident history also significantly affect used-car value. Check actual market prices on used-car platforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

What depreciation method is used?

The declining balance method: Value × (1 - rate)^years. This differs from straight-line depreciation used in accounting.

Will this match the actual used-car market price?

Not necessarily. This is a mathematical estimate. Real market prices depend on supply, demand, condition, and mileage.

Is my data stored?

No. All calculations run locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server.
