💡 Vehicle Tax Tips

  • Non-commercial passenger car acquisition tax rate: 7% (2026 standard)
  • Compact cars (under 1000cc): 4% rate with up to ₩750,000 discount for 1 personal vehicle
  • Used cars: the higher of actual transaction price or standard market value is used as the tax base
  • Electric vehicles follow the same rate structure based on vehicle category

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the compact car discount applied?

For compact cars (under 1000cc) registered for non-commercial use under a personal name (1 vehicle limit), a 4% tax rate applies with up to ₩750,000 deducted from the calculated tax.

How is the tax base determined for used cars?

The higher of the actual transaction price or the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport's standard market value becomes the tax base. You can check the standard value at the Automobile365 website.
