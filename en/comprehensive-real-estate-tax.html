Frequently Asked Questions

Who has to pay this tax?

Anyone whose combined assessed home value as of June 1 exceeds the deduction (KRW 1.2B for a single home, KRW 900M otherwise) is billed each December.

Isn't this double taxation with property tax?

Both taxes apply to the same property, but the property tax already paid is credited against this tax to adjust for the overlap.

Is joint ownership advantageous?

Joint ownership often works out better than sole ownership, since each spouse can claim their own individual deduction.
