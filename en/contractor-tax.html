💡 Tips

  • Business income (3.3%) vs other income (8.8%): continuous/repeated work = business; one-off = other income.
  • Other income under ₩3M/year may be subject to separate taxation — no need to include in annual filing.
  • 3.3% withholding is a prepayment; final tax is determined at annual filing and may be higher or lower.

Frequently Asked Questions

Business income or other income — which applies?

Repeated, continuous work of the same kind = business income (3.3%). One-off or irregular = other income (8.8%). Classification depends on the actual nature of the work.

Where do I get the withholding receipt?

The payer must issue it. You can request it until February of the following year, or check it on Hometax (홈택스).
