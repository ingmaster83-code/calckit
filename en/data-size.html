💡 Data Size Reference

  • 1 byte = 8 bit
  • 1 KB = 1,024 bytes (binary)
  • 1 MB = 1,024 KB = 1,048,576 byte
  • 1 GB = 1,024 MB
  • 1 TB = 1,024 GB

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between GB and GiB?

GB (gigabyte) uses decimal (1,000³); GiB (gibibyte) uses binary (2³⁰ = 1,073,741,824). Hard drive manufacturers use decimal GB, so drives appear slightly smaller than advertised.

How many GB is 1 TB?

1 TB = 1,000 GB (decimal). In binary terms, 1 TB ≈ 931 GiB.

What is the difference between Mbps and MB/s?

Mbps (megabits/second) measures internet speed; MB/s (megabytes/second) measures file transfer speed. 100 Mbps ≈ 12.5 MB/s.
