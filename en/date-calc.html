Frequently Asked Questions

Are leap years handled automatically?

Yes. The calculator uses the JavaScript Date object to correctly handle leap years (Feb 29).

Can I find the number of weeks between two dates?

Divide the number of days by 7. For example, 28 days apart = 4 weeks.

Can I add or subtract a number of days from a date?

Yes. Enter a base date and add or subtract any number of days to calculate a future or past date.
