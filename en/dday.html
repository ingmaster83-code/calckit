Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between D-Day and D+1?

When the reference day is D-0, the next day is D+1. This calculator counts differences including today.

Can I calculate past dates too?

Yes. If the target date is earlier than today, the calculator shows how many days have passed.

Can I use it for birthdays and anniversaries?

Yes. Use it to count down to weddings, exams, birthdays, and any other special occasions.
