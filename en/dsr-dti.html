💡 DSR/DTI Tips

  • DSR = (sum of all annual loan payments) ÷ annual income × 100
  • DTI = (mortgage payment + other loans' interest) ÷ annual income × 100
  • Once total borrowing exceeds KRW 100M, borrower-level DSR caps apply: 40% at banks, 50% at savings banks/tier-2 lenders.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between DSR and DTI?

DTI only counts mortgage principal+interest and other loans' interest. DSR is stricter — it counts principal+interest on every loan you carry, including credit loans and card loans.

When does the 40% DSR cap apply?

Once a borrower's total loan balance exceeds KRW 100M, banks apply a 40% DSR cap and savings banks/tier-2 lenders apply 50%.

How can I lower my DSR?

Extending the loan term reduces the annual payment, and paying off small credit loans first reduces the "other loans" portion of the ratio.
