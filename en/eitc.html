💡 2024 Korea EITC Maximum Benefits

  • Single-person household: up to ₩1,650,000 (income ₩4M–₩9M)
  • Single-earner family: up to ₩2,850,000 (income ₩7M–₩14M)

  • Assets ₩140M–₩200M: 50% reduction applied
  • Assets ₩200M or more: not eligible

Frequently Asked Questions

When can I apply for Korea EITC?

Regular application: May 1–31 each year. Semi-annual application: September 1–15 (first half), and March 1–15 of the following year (second half). Late applications incur a 10% penalty reduction.

How do I apply?

Apply online via Hometax (www.hometax.go.kr) or the Sontax smartphone app. Phone application via ARS 1544-9944 is also available, as is in-person application at local tax offices.

What is the difference between EITC and Child Tax Credit (자녀장려금)?

EITC (근로장려금) supports low-income workers and self-employed individuals. Child Tax Credit (자녀장려금) supports households with children under 18. Both can be applied for and received simultaneously.
