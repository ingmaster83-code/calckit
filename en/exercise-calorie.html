💡 Exercise Calorie Calculator Tips

  • MET (Metabolic Equivalent of Task) measures exercise intensity — higher MET means more calories burned
  • Heavier body weight burns more calories for the same activity
  • Burning 1g of fat requires approximately 7 kcal
  • One bowl of cooked rice (210g) is approximately 300 kcal

Frequently Asked Questions

What is MET?

MET (Metabolic Equivalent of Task) is the ratio of exercise oxygen consumption to resting oxygen consumption. A higher MET indicates a more intense activity. The formula used is: Calories = MET × weight (kg) × time (hours).

Will the result differ from my actual calorie burn?

Yes. Actual calories burned vary by individual factors such as muscle mass, fitness level, and exercise intensity. This calculator uses MET-based averages and is intended as a reference estimate.

Is my data stored or sent to any server?

No. All calculations are performed entirely within your browser and no input data is transmitted to any server. Your information is completely safe.
