Frequently Asked Questions

What is a simplified fraction?

A fraction reduced by its greatest common divisor (GCD) so the numerator and denominator can no longer be divided by the same number.

What's the difference between a mixed number and an improper fraction?

An improper fraction has a numerator greater than or equal to its denominator (e.g. 7/3). A mixed number splits that into a whole number plus a proper fraction (e.g. 2 1/3).

Is my data saved anywhere?
