Frequently Asked Questions

Is 3.3% always withheld?

Yes, for service (freelance) contracts classified as business income, the payer withholds 3.3% before payment.

Can I get a refund?

Freelancers file a comprehensive income tax return in May. After applying deductions, you may receive a refund if you overpaid.
