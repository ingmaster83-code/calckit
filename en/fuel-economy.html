Frequently Asked Questions

How is fuel economy calculated?

Fuel economy (km/L) = Distance (km) ÷ Fuel used (L). Record your odometer reading after each refuel and calculate at the next fill-up.

What is the difference between combined and official fuel economy?

Official fuel economy is measured under standardized government tests; combined fuel economy blends city (45%) and highway (55%) figures. Real-world fuel economy may differ.

Can I also calculate fuel cost?

Fuel cost = Distance ÷ Fuel economy × Fuel price (KRW/L).
