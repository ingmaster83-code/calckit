Frequently Asked Questions

Does the GPA calculation method vary by university?

Yes, different universities use different scales (4.5, 4.3, or 4.0) and may have different grade breakpoints. Always check your university's academic regulations and select the matching scale.

How does retaking an F grade affect GPA?

Most universities replace the original grade with the retaken grade. However, some schools cap the maximum grade for retaken courses (e.g., B+ maximum). Check your university's retake policy.

How do I calculate major and general education GPA separately?

Use the calculator twice — once entering only major courses, and once entering only general education courses. This gives you separate GPA figures for each category.
