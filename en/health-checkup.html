Blood Pressure (mmHg)

※ For reference only — not a medical diagnosis. Consult a doctor for abnormal values.

💡 Tips

  • Blood pressure should be measured at rest. It can temporarily spike right after exercise or under stress.
  • Fasting glucose is measured after 8+ hours of fasting. Do not confuse with post-meal glucose.
  • BMI thresholds use Asian standards (overweight ≥23). Western guidelines use ≥25 for overweight.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use this as a medical diagnosis?

No. This tool is for reference only and does not replace professional medical advice. Please visit a doctor for abnormal values.

Can someone with a normal BMI be unhealthy?

Yes. BMI does not reflect fat distribution. Abdominal obesity and muscle mass should also be considered.

Is my data stored?

No. All calculations run locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server.
