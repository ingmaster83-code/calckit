💡 Korea Wage Tips

  • 2026 minimum wage: KRW 10,320/hr (up KRW 290 from 2025)
  • Weekly holiday pay (주휴수당): 1 paid day off per week for workers ≥15 hrs/week
  • Monthly conversion: 1 month = 4.345 weeks (365 ÷ 7 ÷ 12)
  • Employers who pay below minimum wage face criminal penalties

Frequently Asked Questions

What is weekly holiday pay (주휴수당)?

Weekly holiday pay is a paid weekly day off granted to workers who work 15+ hours per week and attend all scheduled shifts. It applies to part-time and full-time workers alike in Korea.

What is Korea's minimum wage in 2026?

KRW 10,320 per hour. Monthly minimum (40 hr/week including holiday pay) is approximately KRW 2,156,880.

Why 4.345 weeks per month?

365 days ÷ 7 days ÷ 12 months = 4.345 weeks. The Ministry of Employment uses this figure when converting weekly pay to monthly wages.
