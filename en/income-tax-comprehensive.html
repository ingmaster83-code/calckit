Frequently Asked Questions

When is the comprehensive income tax filing period?

May 1–31 each year. Taxpayers subject to faithful reporting confirmation have until June 30.

Do I need to file if I only have employment income?

If you only have employment income and completed year-end tax settlement, separate filing is not required.

Is my data stored?

No. All calculations run locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server.
