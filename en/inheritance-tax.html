💡 Deductions & Rates (2026)

  • Whichever is greater applies: the KRW 500M lump-sum deduction, or (KRW 200M basic deduction + KRW 50M per child).
  • The spouse deduction ranges from a minimum of KRW 500M up to KRW 3B based on the actual inherited amount and statutory share (this calculator applies only the KRW 500M minimum).
  • Rates follow the same 10–50%, 5-tier progressive structure as gift tax.

Frequently Asked Questions

When must inheritance tax be filed?

Within 6 months from the last day of the month in which death occurred.

How much can pass tax-free to a spouse and children?

Combining the KRW 500M lump-sum deduction with the minimum KRW 500M spouse deduction, estates up to roughly KRW 1B are often tax-free — though the exact amount depends on how the estate is divided.

How does inheritance tax differ from gift tax?

Inheritance is a transfer upon death; a gift is a transfer during life. The rate structure is identical, but the deductions and limits differ.
