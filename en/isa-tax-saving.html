💡 Tips

  • ISA benefits apply after the mandatory 3-year holding period (conditions apply for shorter terms).
  • General type: ₩2M tax-free. Low-income/farmer: ₩4M tax-free. Excess taxed at 9.9% separately.
  • Annual contribution limit ₩20M (cumulative ₩100M). Rolling ISA proceeds into a pension account gives additional deductions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between account types?

General type offers ₩2M tax-free; low-income/farmer type offers ₩4M. Low-income eligibility: annual salary ≤₩50M or total income ≤₩38M.

Is the account automatically closed after 3 years?

No. The account stays open after the mandatory period. You can withdraw, close, or keep investing.

Is my data stored?

No. All calculations run in your browser only. No data is sent to any server.
