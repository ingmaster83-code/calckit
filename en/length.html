Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert inches to centimeters?

1 inch = 2.54 cm. For example, 6 inches = 15.24 cm.

How do I convert miles to kilometers?

1 mile ≈ 1.60934 km. Useful for converting US road sign distances to kilometers.

How do I convert feet to meters?

1 foot = 0.3048 m. 170 cm ≈ 5 feet 7 inches.
