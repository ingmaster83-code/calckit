Korea Lotto 6/45 Number Generator 2026

Generate random Lotto 6/45 numbers instantly

💡 Lotto Tips

  • Korea Lotto 6/45: pick 6 numbers from 1–45. Prizes are awarded by how many match the drawn numbers.
  • The odds of winning 1st prize (all 6 numbers) are approximately 1 in 8.1 million.
  • This generator uses the browser's cryptographic random number function (crypto.getRandomValues) for fair results.
  • Purchase lotto tickets at official retailers or the Dong Haeng Bokwon app (m.dhlottery.co.kr).

Frequently Asked Questions

Are computer-generated numbers luckier than hand-picked ones?

No. Lotto is a purely random draw, so every combination has an identical probability (~1 in 8.1 million). A computer-generated set has exactly the same chance as any manual selection.

What do the ball colors mean?

Ball color indicates number range: 1–10 yellow, 11–20 blue, 21–30 red, 31–40 purple, 41–45 grey — matching the official Korea Lotto color scheme.

Is any data stored or uploaded?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server. Your selections remain completely private.
