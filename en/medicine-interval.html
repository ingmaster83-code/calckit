  • Minor delays are fine for most medications, but antibiotics and anticonvulsants require precise timing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I miss a dose by 1–2 hours?

For most medications, take it as soon as you remember and shift subsequent doses accordingly. If the next dose is almost due, skip and resume the regular schedule. Always confirm with your doctor or pharmacist.

Why is exact timing important for antibiotics?

Maintaining steady blood concentration is essential to suppress bacteria effectively. Irregular intervals can lead to antibiotic resistance.
