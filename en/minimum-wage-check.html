💡 Tips

  • Korea's 2026 minimum wage is ₩10,320/hour (2.9% increase from 2025).
  • Monthly salary conversion includes weekly holiday pay. Standard 40-hour week = ~209 hours/month.
  • Employers who pay below minimum wage face up to 3 years imprisonment or ₩20M fine.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does minimum wage apply during probation?

During the first 3 months of probation, up to 10% reduction is allowed. However, simple labor jobs are exempt from this reduction.

What is weekly holiday pay (주휴수당)?

Paid day off granted to workers who work 15+ hours/week. It should be included in monthly salary calculations.

Is my data stored?
