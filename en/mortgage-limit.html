💡 Mortgage Tips

  • LTV: loan amount ÷ property value — capped at 50–70% depending on zone and ownership
  • DSR: all annual debt repayments ÷ annual income must stay within 40%
  • Properties over ₩1.5B in speculative zones: LTV = 0% (mortgage not available)
  • Actual limits depend on lender credit assessment and may differ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which takes priority — LTV or DSR?

Whichever produces the lower limit applies. If the LTV limit is lower, LTV determines your borrowing cap; if the DSR limit is lower, DSR does.

Can owners of 2+ homes get a mortgage?

In speculative overheated or adjustment zones, owners of 2+ homes face an LTV of 0%, effectively blocking mortgages. Non-regulated zones may still allow borrowing within the applicable LTV.

Is my data saved?
