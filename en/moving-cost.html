💡 Tips

  • Peak season (Mar–Apr, Sep–Oct) can add 20–30% to costs. Consider off-season for savings.
  • Full-pack handles everything but costs more. Semi-pack is better value for moderate-sized moves.
  • No elevator? Expect ₩30,000–₩50,000 surcharge per flight of stairs above ground floor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do quotes vary so much?

Volume of belongings, date (weekday/peak season), parking situation, elevator access, and company size all affect pricing. Always get an in-person quote.

Should I provide meals or tips?

Not required by law, but it is customary to offer a meal or ₩50,000–₩200,000 equivalent depending on move size and service level.

Is my data stored?

No. All calculations run locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server.
