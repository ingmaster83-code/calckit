Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between percent and percentage points (pp)?

Percent expresses a ratio; percentage points measure the absolute difference between two percentages. A rise from 2% to 3% is a 1 pp increase but a 50% relative increase.

How do I calculate a discount?

Discount rate (%) = (original price − discounted price) ÷ original price × 100.

How do I calculate a percentage increase?

Percentage increase (%) = (new value − old value) ÷ old value × 100.
