Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 21, 2026

1. Collection and Use of Personal Information

WooaCalc (the "Service") does not require account registration and does not collect any personal information. All data you enter (numbers, dates, etc.) is processed solely within your browser and is never transmitted to any server.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

Some features, such as D-Day saving, use the browser's local storage (localStorage). This data is stored only on your device and is never sent to a server. You can delete it at any time from your browser settings.

3. Third-Party Services

The Service uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense: May use cookies to serve ads. See Google Advertising Policies for details.
  • Google Fonts: Used to serve web fonts.

4. Data Security

All calculation logic runs locally in JavaScript within your browser. No data is transmitted to any external server, eliminating any risk of data leakage.

5. Your Rights

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by clearing your browser's local storage. Advertising cookies can be managed through your browser's cookie settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in law or the Service. Any changes will be announced on this page.

7. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the contact information provided on the Service.
