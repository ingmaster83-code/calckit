💡 Property Tax Tips (2026)

  • Assessed to the owner of record as of June 1 each year, billed in two installments: July (buildings/half of housing) and September (land/remaining half).
  • The single-home discount only applies up to an assessed value of KRW 900M — any amount above that is taxed at standard rates.
  • Jointly-owned homes (e.g. married couples) are judged for single-home status based on the household's total number of homes.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is property tax due?

Bills are issued twice a year, in July and September, each covering half the annual amount. Amounts under KRW 200,000 may be billed in full in July.

How does the single-home discount work?

Single-home owners with an assessed value up to KRW 900M get a reduced fair market ratio (43–45%) and reduced tax rate (0.05–0.35%), lowering their overall burden compared to the standard rate.

Does joint ownership reduce property tax?

Property tax is billed proportionally by ownership share, so the total doesn't change much. However, joint ownership can meaningfully reduce comprehensive real estate tax (종부세) by using each owner's individual deduction.
