Frequently Asked Questions

What is a non-taxable allowance?

Expense reimbursement-type pay such as meal allowances. As of 2026, up to KRW 200,000 per month is excluded from income tax and insurance premium calculations.

Is there a cap on national pension contributions?

Yes. National pension contributions are capped at a monthly income of approximately KRW 5.9 million (2026 estimate). Higher earners pay the same maximum amount.

Do dependents affect my take-home pay?

Each dependent gives you a KRW 1.5 million annual personal deduction, which reduces your income tax and increases take-home pay.
