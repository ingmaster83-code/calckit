Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between simple and compound interest?

Simple interest applies only to the principal; compound interest applies to both principal and accumulated interest. Most Korean savings accounts use simple interest.

How much interest income tax is withheld?

General interest income is subject to 15.4% withholding tax (14% income tax + 1.4% local income tax). Tax-exempt savings products are not subject to this tax.

What happens to interest if I withdraw early?

Early withdrawal applies a lower early termination rate, significantly reducing your interest. Rates vary by institution — check beforehand.
