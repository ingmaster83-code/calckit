Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between DEG and RAD?

DEG calculates angles in degrees, RAD in radians. Most everyday angle calculations use DEG; math and engineering work typically uses RAD.

What's the difference between log and ln?

log is the base-10 common logarithm, ln is the natural logarithm with base e.

Is my data saved anywhere?

No. All calculations happen locally in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
