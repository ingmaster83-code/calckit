💡 Severance Pay Tips

  • Severance = Daily Average Wage × 30 × (Days Employed / 365)
  • Daily Average Wage = Total salary last 3 months / 91 days
  • Severance is prorated even for under 1 year (legally required only for 1+ year continuous employment)
  • Bonuses and annual leave pay are converted to a 3-month basis and included

Frequently Asked Questions

How long must I work to receive severance pay?

You must have worked at least 15 hours per week continuously for 1 or more years. Part-time workers who meet these conditions are also entitled to severance pay.

What is the daily average wage?

Total wages paid in the 3 months before resignation divided by the total calendar days in that period. Bonuses and annual leave pay may be partially included.

By when must severance pay be paid?

Payment must be made within 14 days of the last working day. It can be extended by mutual agreement, but failure to pay violates the Labor Standards Act.
