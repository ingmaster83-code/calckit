Frequently Asked Questions

How is the average cost calculated?

Average cost = Total Investment ÷ Total Shares. Each buy order contributes (shares × price) to the total investment and shares tally.

What is dollar-cost averaging (DCA)?

DCA means buying additional shares at lower prices to reduce your average cost basis. This calculator lets you see exactly how much each new purchase moves your average.

Is my data saved anywhere?

No. All calculations run entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any server.
