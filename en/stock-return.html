Frequently Asked Questions

Is the securities transaction tax charged only on selling?

Yes, the transaction tax is applied only when selling. Both KOSPI and KOSDAQ are unified at 0.18% from 2024.

Is there capital gains tax on Korean stocks?

The Financial Investment Income Tax (금투세) was abolished in 2025. General retail investors (minority shareholders) are not subject to capital gains tax on domestic Korean stocks. Major shareholders (holding over 1 billion KRW per stock) have separate tax rates.
