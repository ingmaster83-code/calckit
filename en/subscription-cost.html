Frequently Asked Questions

Should I include free trials?

Yes — if the trial auto-converts to paid, include it now so you can budget accurately before the charge hits.

How do I decide which to cancel?

List only services you actually used in the past month. Anything you haven't touched is the top cancellation candidate.

Is my data stored?

No. All calculations run locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server.
