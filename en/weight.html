Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert pounds to kilograms?

1 pound ≈ 0.4536 kg. Body weight of 150 lb ≈ 68 kg.

How many grams is an ounce?

1 ounce (oz) ≈ 28.35 g. Commonly used in food recipes and gold weight measurement.

How do I convert don (Korean gold unit) to grams?

1 don (Korean precious metal unit) = 3.75 g. Frequently used when calculating gold prices.
